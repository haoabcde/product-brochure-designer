# Layout rules

Use 14–18 mm safe margins. Keep body text at or above 10 pt and notes at or above 8.5 pt. Show a concise title, then a short explanation. Use available information; do not manufacture extra pages. Page count is 6 with basic data, 8 when modules or outcomes are available, and 10 only with substantive content for venue, value, modules, schedule, outcomes, and specification pages.
