# Style presets

Use `editorial` for general and university products, `technology` for AI/science, `industrial` for company and manufacturing, `heritage` for culture and craft, `ecology` for nature, and `history` for historical themes. A product may specify a preset directly. The `brand.colors` object can replace colors but should remain restrained.

Every deck needs a photo-led cover, a quiet information page, and a change of composition between consecutive pages. Avoid repetitive cards, busy texture, excessive icons, rainbow color, and dense paragraphs.
