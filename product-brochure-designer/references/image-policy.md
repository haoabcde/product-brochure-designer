# Image policy

Prefer client-supplied assets, official photos, then licensed stock. Record original URL or local origin and whether the asset is approved. A normal web image is `preview` and cannot be promoted to delivery. Do not use AI images to represent a real venue, factory, customer activity, product, or person.

Choose one strong image over multiple weak ones. Reject watermarks, visible large text, low resolution, unrelated locations, noisy crowds, heavy saturation, and stretched crops. Use lower saturation and a subtle dark overlay for readable copy.
